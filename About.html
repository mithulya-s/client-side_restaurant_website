Our Story

Established in 1995 in the heart of Provence, Rafale was founded to celebrate the richness of French gastronomy. Chef Jean-Luc Moreau, inspired by the culinary traditions of the region and his expertise from Parisian kitchens, envisioned a space where classic flavors and modern creativity could coexist. From its humble beginnings, Rafale became renowned for its dedication to exceptional dining experiences and attention to detail. Each ingredient is thoughtfully sourced, and every dish reflects a balance of tradition and innovation. Over the years, Rafale has grown into a symbol of Provence's charm and the transformative power of food. Guests return not only for the exquisite flavors but also for the warm and elegant atmosphere that defines every visit.

Each dish reflects our unwavering commitment to quality, combining seasonal ingredients with the artistry of French cuisine. Over the years, our approach has remained consistent—balancing tradition with innovation to deliver an unforgettable dining experience.As we continue to grow, our dedication to crafting refined, heartfelt experiences remains at the center of everything we do.
